Oddly Sourced · Exhibit 002

Why Turkey Stands Still at 9:05 Every November 10

Every orange seal in the video (S1, S2…) points to a row on this page: the sentence it supports, the sources and a short quote from them. Found a source that tells it differently? Tell us in the comments on YouTube.

  1. S1

    Turkey's education ministry tells schools that at five past nine, with a siren, a command or a bugle, everyone stands for two minutes.

    • Millî Eğitim Bakanlığı Bayrak Törenleri Yönergesi, md. 9 — MEB, 2007 (Wayback 2016-12-14)
  2. S2

    Every tenth of November, at five past nine in the morning, sirens sound across Turkey and much of the country stands still.

    • Türkiye'de 10 Kasım Törenleri: "İlk Matem" 10 Kasım 1939'dan … — Hakan Uzun, Çağdaş Türkiye Tarihi Araştırmaları Dergisi XIX/38, 2019
    • Yerel Basında … 10 Kasım Anma Törenleri: Adana, Mersin ve Tarsus (1938-1950) — Oğuz Kalafat, Belgi 23, 2022
  3. S3

    The answer runs backwards, through a doctors' bulletin, a death sentence and a broken pocket watch.

    • Ulus, 10 İkinciteşrin 1938, 2. basış, ön sayfa — Wikimedia Commons, 1938
  4. S4

    And the man those newspapers mourned had been sentenced to death by the government he once served.

    • Damat Ferit Paşa’nın IV. Hükümeti Döneminde Kuvâ-yı Milliye İleri Gelenleri Hakkında Verilen İdam Kararları — Osman Akandere, Atatürk Yolu 43, 2009
    • Dürrizâde Abdullah — Sinan Başaran, Atatürk Ansiklopedisi, n.d.

    “10 Nisan’da Kuvay-i Milliye önderlerini “âsi” olarak suçlayan bir hükûmet bildirisi”

  5. S5

    On August 10th, at Chunuk Bair, a piece of shrapnel hit him in the chest.

    • Conkbayırı’nda Atatürk’ün Hayatını Kurtaran Saat — Mevlüt Çelebi, Belgi 16, 2018
    • The Place where Atatürk’s Watch was Smashed — Çanakkale Savaşları Gelibolu Tarihi Alan Başkanlığı, n.d.

    “the watch on his right breast saved him”

  6. S6

    The silence is for one man, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, the founder and first president of the Turkish Republic.

    • Atatürk, Gazi Mustafa Kemal (1881-10 Kasım 1938) — Şerafettin Turan, Atatürk Ansiklopedisi (AAM), n.d.
  7. S7

    By regulation, flags on official buildings fly at half-mast every tenth of November, and the flag at his mausoleum in Ankara is lowered on that day alone.

    • Türk Bayrağı Tüzüğü — Bakanlar Kurulu Kararı 85/9034, Resmî Gazete 18697, 1985 (mevzuat.gov.tr, Wayback 2015-02-20)
  8. S8

    And not everything was left to choice: the programme for a ceremony in Mersin that year made black clothing compulsory.

    • Yerel Basında … 10 Kasım Anma Törenleri: Adana, Mersin ve Tarsus (1938-1950) — Oğuz Kalafat, Belgi 23, 2022
  9. S9

    It said the President had died in a deep coma, on Thursday morning, at five past nine, at Dolmabahçe Palace in Istanbul.

    • Atatürk ve Dolmabahçe Sarayı — N. Yazıcı, E. Kısıklı, Atatürk Ansiklopedisi, n.d.
  10. S10

    His aide and childhood friend, Salih Bozok, shot himself on hearing of his death.

    • Salih Bozok (1881-1941) — Fahri Maden, Atatürk Ansiklopedisi, n.d.
  11. S11

    Florya · 1937 · His aide, Salih Bozok

    • Anılması Gereken Bir Kitap: Geometri — O. Tuncay Başkaya, Bilim ve Teknoloji, 2013 (Atılım Üniv. açık arşiv kaydı)

    Surnames came to Turkey by law in 1934.

    • Soyadı Kanunu — Oğuz Aytepe, Atatürk Ansiklopedisi, n.d.

    On November 24th, 1934, every member of parliament present voted to give him a surname of his own.

    • 1934 Tarihli Soy Adı Kanunu Uygulamaları ve Malatya Örneği — Mehmet Korkud Aydın, Belgi 18, 2019

    It was Atatürk.

    • Kemal öz atlı Cümhur Reisimize verilen soy adı hakkında kanun (Kanun 2587) — Resmî Gazete 2865, 1934, Vikikaynak transkripsiyonu
    • Soyadı Kanunu — Oğuz Aytepe, Atatürk Ansiklopedisi, n.d.
